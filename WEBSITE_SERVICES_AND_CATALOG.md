Providing sterile, flush, airtight architectural enclosures compliant with **cGMP, WHO-GMP, Schedule M, and ISO 14644**.

* **Modular Wall Panels:** 
  * 50mm / 80mm / 100mm PUF (Polyurethane Foam) insulated sandwich panels.
  * Skin materials: Pre-Painted Galvanized Iron (PPGI), Pre-Painted Galvalume (PPGL), or Stainless Steel (SS 304).
  * Non-thermal break aluminum extrusion profiles with interlocking tongue-and-groove jointing.
* **Modular Ceiling Systems:**
  * Walkable and non-walkable ceiling panels with integrated suspension hardware for overhead maintenance.
  * Flush cutouts for HEPA filters, light fixtures, and inspection hatches.
* **Cleanroom Doors & Vision Panels:**
  * Single-leaf and double-leaf flush metal doors with perimeter silicone seals, drop seals, and push plates.
  * Interlocking door controllers (airlock systems) to prevent cross-contamination.
  * Double-glazed flush view panels (toughened safety glass with silica gel desiccant to prevent fogging).
* **Coving & Architectural Trims:**
  * Extruded aluminum and PVC covings for seamless wall-to-floor, wall-to-ceiling, and 3D corner transitions to eliminate 90° dust-trapping edges.
* **Specialized Cleanroom Flooring:**
  * Self-leveling anti-static epoxy and Polyurethane (PU) flooring resistant to chemical washdowns and heavy wheel traffic.

### DIVISION 2: HVAC & Air Handling Systems (Design, Supply & Erection)

Engineered air-management solutions designed as per **ISHRAE, ASHRAE, and ISO** cleanroom classifications.

* **Air Handling Units (AHU):**
  * Custom Double Skin AHUs with thermal-break and non-thermal-break extruded aluminum profiles.
  * Outer skin PPGI / Inner skin SS 304 or GI with high-density PUF insulation.
  * Energy-efficient backward curved plug fans, EC fans, and VFD-ready motors.
  * Integrated multi-stage filtration tracks (Pre-filter + Fine filter sections).
* **Dehumidification Air Handling Units (DAHU):**
  * Desiccant rotor dehumidifiers specifically engineered for low-humidity pharmaceutical manufacturing (< 20% to 35% RH).
  * Essential for hygroscopic formulations: effervescent tablets, soft gelatin capsules, dry syrup powder filling, and packaging halls.
* **Coil Technology & Heat Transfer:**
  * High-efficiency Chilled Water (CHW) Coils, Hot Water (HW) Coils, and Direct Steam Coils.
  * Copper tubes with aluminum fins / hydrophilic fins / SS tubes for corrosive environments.
* **DX Units & VRV/VRF Systems:**
  * Direct Expansion (DX) condensing units for standalone zones.
  * Design, supply, piping, repair, and overhaul of multi-split VRV/VRF commercial systems.
* **Ventilation & Air Movement:**
  * Fan Coil Units (FCU), Air Washers, Heat Recovery Units (HRU - Energy recovery heat wheels and plate exchangers).
* **GI Ducting & Acoustic/Thermal Insulation:**
  * Factory-fabricated Galvanized Iron (GI) ducting (Class 4 / Class 8 as per SMACNA guidelines).
  * Thermal insulation using closed-cell Nitrile rubber, XLPE foam, and high-density Glass Wool with aluminum foil facing.
  * Acoustic lining for noise attenuation in critical testing labs and sound-sensitive zones.

### DIVISION 5: Industrial Water Treatment Systems (RO & ETP Plants)

Pharma and chemical manufacturing facilities require pure process water for formulation and compliant effluent management for environmental clearance.

* **Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plants:**
  * Multi-stage purified water generation systems engineered for high recovery and low fouling.
  * Pre-treatment stages: Multi-Grade Sand Filter (MGF), Activated Carbon Filter (ACF), and Automated Dual Media Softeners.
  * Micron cartridge filtration (5µ and 1µ) followed by high-pressure composite RO membranes.
  * Integrated Ultra-Filtration (UF) and UV Disinfection units.
  * Direct utility for tablet/capsule processing, cleanroom equipment cleaning, and boiler feed water.
* **Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) & Wastewater Systems:**
  * Complete turnkey ETP plants engineered to meet State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) and Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) discharge limits.
  * Processes: Equalization, flash mixing, chemical coagulation, flocculation, primary/secondary clarifiers, and activated sludge biological digestion.
  * Tertiary treatment: Sand filtration, activated carbon polishing, and ozone/chlorination disinfection.
  * Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) integration with multi-effect evaporators (MEE) and filter presses for sludge dewatering.

### DIVISION 7: Automation, BMS / EMS & Regulatory Validation / Qualification

* **Building Management (BMS) & Environmental Monitoring (EMS):**
  * Centralized SCADA and PLC control architecture for total plant monitoring.
  * Real-time multi-channel logging of Differential Pressure ($\Delta P$), Temperature, Relative Humidity (%RH), and particle count trends.
  * **21 CFR Part 11 Compliance:** Tamper-proof electronic records, automated audit trails, password protection, and digital signatures required for USFDA and international inspections.
* **Testing, Qualification & Validation Services (GDP Compliant):**
  * **HVAC Validation Protocols:** Design Qualification (DQ), Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ).
  * **Airflow & Air Changes Testing:** Air velocity measurement, CFM flow profiling, and calculation of Air Changes Per Hour (ACPH).
  * **Room Differential Pressure Mapping:** Verification of room-to-room pressure cascading (positive vs. negative pressure suites).
  * **HEPA Filter Integrity Testing:** In-situ DOP / PAO aerosol challenge testing with calibrated aerosol photometers.
  * **Airborne Particle Counting:** Cleanroom ISO classification from ISO Class 5 (Grade A / Class 100) to ISO Class 8 (Grade D / Class 100,000) under "At Rest" and "In Operation" states.
  * **Thermal & Environmental Mapping:** Multi-point sensor data logging for cleanrooms, cold storage rooms, incubators, and stability chambers.
  * **Utility Testing:** Pure Steam validation (dryness fraction, superheat, non-condensable gases), and Compressed Dry Air / Nitrogen particulate and oil vapor testing.
